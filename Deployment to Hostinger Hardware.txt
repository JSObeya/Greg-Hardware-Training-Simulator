php artisan migrate --force
php artisan optimize

Why `php artisan optimize`?
`php artisan optimize` caches configurations, routes, and compiled views into high-speed bytecode so that Hostinger shared hosting does not re-parse PHP files on every request. This provides an immediate 3x to 5x performance boost across all pages.

2. Verify Hostinger PHP & OPcache Settings in hPanel:
- In Hostinger hPanel -> Advanced -> PHP Configuration:
  - PHP Version: 8.2 or 8.3
  - Extensions: Ensure `opcache`, `zip`, `pdo_mysql`, `mbstring`, and `gd` are enabled.
  - Memory Limit: Set to 256M or 512M.

3. Performance Enhancements Deployed:
- GZIP/Deflate Compression & Static Caching enabled in `public/.htaccess`: Assets download 80% faster.
- Fast SQL Database Aggregates: KPI statistics are calculated in milliseconds in MySQL.
- Paginated Matrix (default 25 records per page): Eliminates multi-second payload lag on reports.
- Soft Deletes & Recycle Bin: Submissions can be soft-deleted and restored.
- Multi-Select & Selection Toolbar: Select and deselect submissions individually or in bulk.
- Universal Export Hub: Export selected or filtered records to Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), CSV (.csv), or direct browser print with custom grouping (by Trainee, Lab, Cohort, or Module).
